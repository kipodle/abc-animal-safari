Privacy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This website is made for kids, and for the parents who play along. Here is what we collect and why, in plain language.

Sign-up for the game

To play the ABC game, a parent enters an email address and a country. We use the email only to share news about new games and stories. We never sell or share it. The country helps us understand where players are from. These details are stored in a private spreadsheet that only the site owner can open.

Children should ask a grown-up to fill in the sign-up. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Letter adventure maker

When you make a letter adventure, the name, age, letter, world and photo you enter are used only inside your own browser to build the book and the PDF. They are not sent to us, uploaded or saved anywhere, and they are gone when you close the page. Your child's photo never leaves your device.

Information saved on your device

The game saves your level, stars, sound setting and the email you used in your own browser (local storage) so you can pick up where you left off. This stays on your device. It is not sent to us, and clearing your browser data removes it.

Visitor statistics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits, countries, devices and which pages are popular. It does not use cookies and does not follow people around the web.

Fonts and links

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and the PDF maker loads its tools from the cdnjs code library, which means your browser contacts those services to download them. Your child's name and photo are not part of those requests. Links to YouTube take you to a site we do not control.

Ads

There are no ads on this website.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your email at any time by contacting us through our YouTube channel. We will remove it from our list.

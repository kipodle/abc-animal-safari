A friendly space for parents

Chat with other moms and dads about motherhood, fatherhood, skincare and self-care, babies and toddlers, school years, health and everything in between. It is only for logged-in members, so every family here has signed up as a parent or guardian.

Choose a nickname

Other parents will see this nickname instead of your email. Please do not use your real full name or your child's name.

Community guidelines
  • Be kind. Every family is different and there is no one right way.
  • This is not medical advice. For anything about health, skin or your child's wellbeing, please ask a doctor, nurse or pharmacist.
  • Keep private things private: no children's names or photos, no addresses, schools or phone numbers.
  • No selling or promoting, and at most one link per post.
  • Press Report on anything that worries you. The site owner reviews reports and can remove posts.
